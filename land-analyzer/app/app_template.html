All-in estimate = asking price + rough cost to bring in water, power and septic, build a driveway and prepare a building pad, using nearby well depths, distance to the nearest home, USDA soil ratings and slope. Planning numbers, not quotes — change them under "Cost assumptions" on any parcel.
